How to Import Your Existing Students:

  • Click Download Sample Excel Template above to download KHS_Existing_Students_Template.xlsx (A copy is also saved in your app folder: c:\Users\suman\Downloads\KHS Billing\KHS_Existing_Students_Template.xlsx).
  • Fill in student records (Admission No, Name, Class, Day Scholar / Residential, Parent Phone).
  • Fill in Past_Amount_Already_Paid if a student has already paid fee this term/year — the system will automatically calculate their exact remaining balance due.
  • Upload the completed file below. Existing students with duplicate admission numbers will be safely skipped.
